Whale Flow — Smart Money vs Crowd venue evidence

What big players are doing on this venue — no wallet guessing. Every trade on the tape is split into tiers: whale ($100k+ clips), retail (under $10k), and the middle. The headline is whale direction (bias +1 = all buying, −1 = all selling) and the bars show their net flow per 15 minutes. The most information-rich read is the divergence: whales buying what retail is selling = classic bottom behavior; whales selling into retail buying = classic top. Book depth and retail positioning are context — walls can be faked, and whales are usually the other side of a crowded crowd.

Model Scorecard the model grades itself

Every reading the engine has ever taken is checked against what price actually did next — 4 hours, 1 day, and 3 days later. Rows show the median forward move when a signal was active, the hit-rate of its thesis, and the sample size; everything is compared against the same-period baseline so a drifting market can't fake an edge. Dimmed rows have too few samples to trust yet. This card is how thresholds stop being opinions: it starts out empty and becomes evidence as the system runs.
